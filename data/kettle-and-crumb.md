# Kettle & Crumb: cafe facts for Kit

## About
Kettle & Crumb is a neighbourhood cafe and bakery at 14 Orchard Row, Millbrook.
We roast our own coffee beans in small batches every Tuesday.
Our house blend is Orchard Row, a chocolate and red apple blend from Colombia and Ethiopia.

## Open hours
Open hours: Monday to Friday: 7 am to 6 pm; Saturday: 8 am to 5 pm; Sunday: 8 am to 2 pm.
We are closed on public holidays, and the app shows the next closed day on the home tab.
The kitchen serves breakfast until 11:30 am every day, then lunch until 3 pm.

## Menu
Coffee menu: Espresso: $3.00; Americano: $3.50; Flat white: $4.20; Cappuccino: $4.20; Latte: $4.50; Mocha: $4.90.
Tea and more: English breakfast tea: $3.20; Earl grey tea: $3.20; Chai: $4.40; Matcha: $4.80; Hot chocolate: $4.20.
Breakfast menu: Sourdough toast with butter: $5.50; Avocado toast: $11.00; Bacon and egg roll: $9.50; Granola bowl: $8.50.
Lunch menu: Chicken and pesto toastie: $12.50; Roast pumpkin salad: $13.00; Soup of the day with bread: $10.50.
Bakery: Butter croissant: $3.80; Almond croissant: $4.60; Cinnamon bun: $4.20; Banana bread: $4.00.
Milk options: whole, skim, oat, almond and soy.
Oat, almond and soy milk cost 60 cents extra.
Decaf is available for every coffee at no extra cost.
Every drink comes in regular or large, and large is 80 cents more.

## Ordering in the app
Order ahead in the Kettle & Crumb app and pick up from the shelf with your name on it.
Your order is usually ready in 10 minutes, or 20 minutes before 9 am on weekdays.
The app takes card, phone wallet and gift card payments; we do not take cash for app orders.
At the counter we take cash, card and phone payments.
You can cancel an app order for a full refund until the barista starts making it.
Delivery is not offered; every order is pick up only.

## Loyalty
Get a free coffee after every 9 coffees with the loyalty card in the app.
Bring your own cup and get 30 cents off any hot drink.
Gift cards come in $20, $50 and $100 and never expire.

## Diet and allergens
Gluten free options: banana bread, granola bowl and orange polenta cake.
Vegan options: avocado toast, any drink with oat or soy milk, and the granola bowl with coconut yogurt.
All our food is made in a kitchen that uses nuts, wheat, eggs and dairy, so we cannot promise allergen free food.

## The space
Free wifi: the network is KettleGuest and the password is printed on your receipt.
You can bring your dog to the front terrace, and we keep a water bowl by the door.
There is step free entry and an accessible toilet at the back.
We take table bookings for groups of 6 or more by phone on 555 0142.
Catering boxes for office meetings need 48 hours notice and start at $65 for 10 people.
