# Seabrook Family Dental: clinic facts for Kit

## About
Seabrook Family Dental is a small family dental clinic at 220 Harbour Road, Seabrook.
The clinic has 3 dentists, 2 hygienists and 4 treatment rooms.

## Open hours
Open hours: Monday to Thursday: 8 am to 6 pm; Friday: 8 am to 4 pm; Saturday: 9 am to 1 pm; Sunday: closed.
The clinic is closed on public holidays.

## Treatments and prices
Treatment prices: Checkup and clean: $145; New patient exam: $220; Tooth coloured filling: from $180; Root canal: from $950; Crown: from $1,450.
Teeth whitening costs $490 for one 90 minute visit and a take home top up kit.
A hygienist clean takes 45 minutes and includes a fluoride treatment.
The new patient exam includes x-rays and a full check of your teeth and gums.
Checkups for children under 12 cost $75, and we see children from age 2.
Invisible aligners start with a free 30 minute consult and a 3D scan.

## Booking
Book an appointment online at any time, or call the front desk on 555 0187 during open hours.
New patients should arrive 15 minutes early to fill in a short health form.
We send a text reminder 2 days before every appointment.
A late cancellation fee of $50 applies if you cancel your appointment less than 24 hours ahead.
If you are more than 15 minutes late, we may need to move your visit to another day.

## Emergencies
For a dental emergency during open hours, call 555 0187 and we will see you the same day.
For a dental emergency after hours, call the Seabrook after hours dental line on 555 0199.
A knocked out adult tooth should go back in its socket or into milk, and you should call us at once.

## Payment and health funds
Payment: card, phone payments and bank transfer; we do not take cheques.
We claim from your health fund on the spot, so you only pay the gap.
Payment plans: treatment over $1,000 can be split into 4 monthly payments with no interest.

## Comfort and access
Nervous patients can ask for happy gas for $95 per visit.
Free parking: 12 spaces behind the clinic, with the entry on Gull Lane.
The clinic has step free access, a lift to the first floor and a hearing loop at the front desk.
Bus route 40 stops outside the clinic every 15 minutes.
